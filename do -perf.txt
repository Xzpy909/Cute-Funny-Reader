One “review upload” sheet instead of stacked dialogs. Show what will be created, with stray images and archives as toggles.
Library-type vocabulary in one place. Mostly done (one word table per library type); a few shared texts still speak comics, e.g. the drop hint “Chapters are sorted into series”.
