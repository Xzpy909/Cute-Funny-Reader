[ ] Lossless option for PNG / BMP / TIFF (RAW) series — use WebP LOSSLESS, not AVIF.
    Measured 2026-11 on library pages (pixels decoded, then re-encoded losslessly; size vs optimized PNG):
        WebP lossless 69%  ·  AVIF lossless 97%  ·  JPEG XL lossless 59%
      - AVIF's lossless mode barely beats PNG. Pillow can't even make true lossless AVIF
        (quality 100 still changes pixels); it needs libavif's identity-matrix mode.
      - WebP lossless via Pillow (lossless=True, method=6) is pixel-exact, and every browser shows it.
        Slow-ish: ~6 s per 1 MP page at method 6 — fine for the background queue.
      - JPEG XL is smaller still, but not every browser shows it (the reader would have to transcode it
        when you read) — so not the default.
      - Lossless is only for lossless sources: re-encoding a JPEG / AVIF losslessly makes it 5–8x BIGGER.
        Keep the rule: a page whose new version isn't smaller keeps its original.
